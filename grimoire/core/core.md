# Core

> Loaded into context at every boot. Keep it short - every line here costs
> tokens in every session.

Who the user is: name, what they do, what they are working on. A few lines the
program should always know without asking. Fill this in; the file ships empty.

A save appends a `## Section/topic` block below with the surface of that topic -
what it is, what stage it is at, what is still open - after showing you the exact
lines and waiting for your y/N. Contents belong in your own notes, not here.
